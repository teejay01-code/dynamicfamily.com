WHO WE ARE

About DFMI

Dynamic Family Missions International is a family-focused ministry based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. We exist to equip parents, strengthen marriages, and raise children in faith — through teaching, counsel, and community programs that bring families closer to God and to one another.

01

Faith-centered homes

Helping families build daily life around shared belief and prayer.

02

Restored relationships

Supporting couples and families through counsel and reconciliation.

03

Raised-up generations

Discipling children and youth to carry faith and character forward.

Our Mission

To strengthen families in Port Harcourt and beyond through faith-based teaching, counsel, and community programs.

Our Vision
